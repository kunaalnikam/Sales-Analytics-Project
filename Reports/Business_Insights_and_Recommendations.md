## 7. Business Insights & Recommendations

### Key Business Insights

**1. Electronics is the Leading Sales Category**

Electronics generated ₹14.47 crore in sales, contributing approximately 73.1% of total revenue.

**Business Impact:** The Electronics category is the primary revenue driver of the business.

**Recommendation:** Monitor Electronics inventory and product availability carefully. Identify the best-selling products within this category to support inventory planning.

**2. High Customer Sales Concentration**

The top 10 customers contribute approximately 84.33% of total sales.

**Business Impact:** The business has a high concentration of revenue among its top customers.

**Recommendation:** Focus on retaining major customers while developing strategies to attract new customers and diversify the customer base.

**3. Furniture Has the Highest Profit Margin**

Furniture has a profit margin of 14.79%, compared with 14.05% for Electronics.

**Business Impact:** Furniture generates a higher profit percentage despite having lower total sales than Electronics.

**Recommendation:** Analyze the most profitable Furniture products and explore opportunities to increase their sales without reducing margins.

**4. Overall Business Profitability**

The business generated ₹2.80 crore in profit on total sales of ₹19.79 crore, resulting in a profit margin of 14.13%.

**Business Impact:** The business is generating positive profit across its sales operations.

**Recommendation:** Monitor profit margins by category and product to identify opportunities for improving overall profitability.

### Summary

The analysis highlights Electronics as the main revenue driver, Furniture as the highest-margin category, and customer concentration as an important area to monitor. These findings can support better inventory planning, customer retention, and profitability management.
